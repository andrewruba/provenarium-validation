The dilution equation

C₁V₁ = C₂V₂
C₁
Stock concentration
V₁
Stock volume to transfer
C₂
Target concentration
V₂
Final preparation volume

Diluent volume = final volume − stock volume.

Before you calculate

The target concentration must be no greater than the stock concentration.

Concentrations and final volume must be positive.

A displayed or exported volume of 0.000 mL can represent a positive volume below 0.0005 mL. A rounded zero does not by itself establish that no transfer is needed.

1 mg/mL = 1,000 µg/mL
1 mL = 1,000 µL
