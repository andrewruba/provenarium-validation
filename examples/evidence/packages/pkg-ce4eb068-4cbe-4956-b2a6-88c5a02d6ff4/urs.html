Synthetic sample register requirements

URS-SAMPLE-REGISTER · Revision 1

Intended use

Demonstration laboratory staff record receipt information, calculate a simple mass-to-volume concentration, and export a receipt register. The application does not determine sample suitability or make clinical decisions.

Operators enter synthetic sample records. Supervisors may approve records. This specification defines intended behavior independently of the example application's implementation. Public demonstration accounts contain no real data.

Roles: operator, supervisor

Access and permissions

URS-AUTH-001 — Active account login and logout

An active account shall sign in using its correct username and password. The application shall display the account identity and role. Sign out shall remove access to retained records until another successful login.

URS-AUTH-002 — Denied login cases

Login shall be denied for an incorrect password belonging to a known active account, a nonexistent username, and a disabled account with its otherwise correct password. Each case shall show Login denied and shall not establish an authenticated session.

URS-AUTH-003 — Signed-out access

A signed-out visitor shall be redirected to Sign in when opening retained records. The records HTTP interface shall return status 401 and no records.

URS-PERM-001 — Supervisor approval

An operator shall not see the Approve sample action and shall receive status 403 when submitting an approval request through the HTTP interface. A supervisor shall be able to approve an existing sample through the browser. The approved state shall persist when the records page is reopened.

Sample receipt and calculation

URS-REC-001 — Receipt fields and invalid input

An operator shall enter a sample using the fields below. Missing required values, a negative mass, or zero volume shall prevent saving and identify the rejected field. A zero mass with a positive volume shall be accepted.

URS-REC-002 — Duplicate prevention

Saving a Sample ID already present in the register shall be rejected with an explanation. Leading and trailing spaces shall be ignored and identifiers shall be case-sensitive. The original record shall remain unchanged and no additional record shall be created.

URS-REC-003 — Retained records

A saved record shall retain the entered Sample ID, Storage location, Receipt date, Mass, and Volume. These values shall remain visible after navigating away and reopening Retained records. The table shall contain one row per saved record and the headers defined for the export.

URS-CALC-001 — Concentration

Concentration in mg/mL shall equal mass in mg divided by volume in mL. The displayed result shall be rounded to three decimal places. Reference cases 12 mg / 3 mL = 4.000 mg/mL and 0 mg / 1 mL = 0.000 mg/mL shall agree within an absolute tolerance of 0.0005 mg/mL. Invalid inputs shall not produce a saved result.

Rationale: Independently specified arithmetic examples, not outputs copied from the application.

Exports and attachments

URS-EXP-001 — Export contents

Download receipt CSV shall produce a UTF-8 CSV file containing one row for each retained sample, ordered by Receipt date then Sample ID. Column order shall be Sample ID, Storage location, Receipt date, Mass (mg), Volume (mL), Concentration (mg/mL), Status. Values shall agree with the retained records, with concentration shown to three decimal places.

URS-FILE-001 — Text attachment retention

An authenticated user shall be able to upload a plain-text .txt attachment of at most 100 KiB. The application shall identify the retained filename and display the retained text without changing its content. Unsupported filenames or oversized attachments shall be rejected.

Installation observations and external evidence

URS-IQ-001 — Reachable application

The target health interface shall return status 200 and identify the application as Synthetic sample register.

URS-IQ-002 — Deployment recovery evidence

The deployment owner shall supply a documented procedure to retain and restore the sample data directory. A reviewer shall verify that the procedure identifies the directory, backup responsibility, and restore verification. Browser observations alone do not establish recovery.
